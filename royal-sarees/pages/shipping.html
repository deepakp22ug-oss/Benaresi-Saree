Return Policy

✓ 30-Day Return Policy: We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy with your saree, we offer a hassle-free return within 30 days of delivery.

Return Eligibility

  • ✓ Saree must be unworn and unwashed
  • ✓ Original tags and labels must be intact
  • ✓ Saree must be returned in original packaging
  • ✓ Return initiated within 30 days of delivery
  • ✓ No damage or stains on the fabric

Return Process

  1. Initiate Return: Contact our support team via WhatsApp, email, or phone
  2. Get RMA Number: You'll receive a return authorization number
  3. Pack & Ship: Pack the saree securely in original packaging
  4. Submit Return: Send using the provided prepaid return label (if available)
  5. Inspection: We'll inspect the returned saree
  6. Refund: Refund processed within 5-7 business days
💳 Refund Method: Refunds will be processed to the original payment method used for purchase.

Exchange Policy

Customs & Import Duties

For international shipments, please note:

Customs Duty: Customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or import fees levied by their country. These costs are the buyer's responsibility and are not included in the shipping cost.

We will declare the item's actual value on the customs form. Delivery delays due to customs clearance are beyond our control, but we'll keep you updated throughout the process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I track my order?

Yes! You'll receive a tracking number via email as soon as your order ships. You can track your package in real-time on the courier's website or through our website.

What if my package is lost?

We work with reliable courier partners with excellent track records. If your package is lost, we'll file a claim and either send a replacement or provide a full refund within 15 days.

Can I change my address after placing an order?

You can change the delivery address if your order hasn't been shipped yet. Contact support immediately with your order number and new address. If already shipped, you'll need to refuse the delivery and we can reshop to the correct address.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes! We offer premium gift wrapping for special occasions. Select this option during checkout. Your saree will be beautifully wrapped and include a personalized gift message if you'd like.

What if I want to return during the sale period?

Sale items are also covered by our 30-day return policy. As long as the saree is unworn and unwashed, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the discounted price.

Can I return internationally?

Yes, international returns are accepted. You'll need to cover the return shipping cost, but we'll provide you with our return address. Once received and inspected, we'll refund the purchase amount.
