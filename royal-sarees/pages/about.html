The VINARM BANARSI RITHEE Story

VINARM BANARSI RITHEE was founded with a singular vision: to bring the timeless elegance of authentic Banarasi sarees to the world while honoring the master artisans who have perfected this craft over three centuries.

Every saree in our collection is handwoven by skilled craftsmen in the sacred city of Banaras, using traditional techniques that have been passed down through generations. We work directly with these artisans, ensuring fair wages and preserving their livelihoods.

Our commitment is not just to provide premium sarees, but to tell the story of each piece – the heritage, the craftsmanship, and the pride of Indian textile traditions.

Banaras Weaving Tradition

Banaras, the ancient city of light, has been the epicenter of Indian textile excellence for over 300 years. The city's master weavers have created some of the world's most exquisite fabrics, with sarees being their crown jewel.

The Banarasi saree represents the pinnacle of Indian textile art. Each saree is a masterpiece that takes weeks or even months to complete, with every thread carefully placed by hand using traditional handlooms.

The intricate zari work (gold thread embroidery), the finest silks, and the timeless motifs make each Banarasi saree a precious heirloom that can be passed down through generations.

Our Craftsmanship

Every saree that bears the VINARM BANARSI RITHEE name is a testament to uncompromising quality. We source only the finest materials: pure silk, premium zari, and the highest grade fabrics available.

Our artisans work with meticulous attention to detail, ensuring that every weave, every pattern, and every embellishment meets our exacting standards. The average Banarasi saree requires 2-4 months of dedicated handwork.

We believe that true luxury is not about price, but about the care, skill, and passion invested in creating something extraordinary. That's what sets VINARM BANARSI RITHEE apart.
